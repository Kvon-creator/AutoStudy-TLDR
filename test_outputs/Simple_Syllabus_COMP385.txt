COMP385: Theory of Computing
============================

Source: Simple_Syllabus_COMP385.pdf
Course prerequisites: COMP360

COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course is the study of topics that include the theory of finite state machines and
automata; regular expressions; Turing machines; grammars; parsing; the language
hierarchy; machine design and construction; computability; insolvability; and the
halting problem. The course also discusses issuses in the equivalence of various
computational models and characterizations. Prerequisites: COMP 360.(F;S;SS)
